Why TENET

Not a case study. A live problem.

Most competitions hand you a decade-old case. TENET hands you a problem our sponsors haven't solved yet — and puts your answer in front of the people who'll act on it.

01

A real, unresolved brief

The problem statement comes straight from our knowledge partner, Zerodha — not a textbook.

02

A panel that can hire you

Judges include the people behind the problem statement, with placement conversations on the table for standout teams.

03

A national stage

Finalist teams converge on ISB Hyderabad for a live, high-pressure final pitch.
